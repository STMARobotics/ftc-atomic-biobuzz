Takeaway

65–80° covers about 15 more points of the field with shoot-on-the-move than 60–75° and leaves far less of the field with no shot. The top end of the hood matters more than the bottom end. Suggested RPM presets for 65–80°: 3090, 3360, 3730. For 60–75°: 3340, 3780.
